What we ask of members

  1. 01

    Be kind and assume good intent

    Everyone here is still learning. Ask, answer and disagree the way you would want to be treated.

  2. 02

    Share learning, not sales pitches

    Post what you tried, what worked and what did not. Offers and promotions stay out of the group.

  3. 03

    Never post client data or anything confidential

    Remove names, figures and anything private before you share an example or a screenshot.

  4. 04

    Credit other people’s work and ideas

    When a prompt, template or idea came from someone else, say so and name them.
