Who it’s for

  • Past and future workshop participants
  • Founders and professionals applying AI in their work
  • Anyone who wants practical, human-first conversations about AI

What you get

  • A place to share what you built and ask what to try next
  • First word on new workshops, events and resources
  • Encouragement from people learning the same things

What we ask of members

  • Be kind and assume good intent
  • Share learning, not sales pitches
  • Never post client data or anything confidential
  • Credit other people’s work and ideas
