Before you request the kit

Is it really free?

Yes. No card is required.

Do I need coding skills?

No. The kit focuses on choosing and planning a workflow.

Do I need a paid AI account?

You can complete the planning worksheets without one. Testing a workflow may require a tool account; check that tool’s current terms.

Will this build an automated system for me?

No. It helps you plan and test a first workflow. Implementation needs depend on your use case.

Will I receive marketing emails?

Only if you choose the optional updates checkbox.

Your first step does not need another tool. It needs a clear task.
