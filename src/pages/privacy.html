Privacy Notice

Draft for owner and legal review. This notice describes how the website is designed to handle personal information. It must be reviewed and approved by Faith Works PH and an authorized reviewer, with the legal entity and privacy contact completed, before the site goes live.

Version: draft 0.1 · Last updated: to be set at approval

Who we are

This website, faithph.ai, is operated by Faith Works PH (legal entity name and registered address to be confirmed). For privacy questions, contact privacy contact to be confirmed or email ibelieve@faithph.ai.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in a form: such as your name, email address, role, company, event details or message, depending on the form.
  • Request context: the page you submitted from, campaign tags in the link you followed (UTM values), the offer or event you chose, the time of your request and your marketing choice.
  • Basic usage information: if analytics are enabled, anonymous information about pages viewed and buttons used. We never send your name, email or message to analytics.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information, and we ask you not to include it in messages.

How we use it

  • To provide what you asked for: deliver a resource, respond to an inquiry, arrange a demo or tell you about dates you asked to hear about.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you ticked the optional updates box. Requesting a resource or sending an inquiry does not sign you up for marketing.
  • To keep records of your consent and to prevent duplicate or spam submissions.

Where it is stored

Form submissions are stored in our customer relationship management system (provider to be confirmed, expected to be GoHighLevel) and are accessible only to the people responsible for responding.

How long we keep it

Retention periods to be confirmed by Faith Works PH.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in each email. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing. Contact us using the details above.

Changes to this notice

We will update the date and version above when this notice changes.
