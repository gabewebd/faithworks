AI is changing your work.
Nobody showed you how.

Most professionals already have access to AI tools. What is missing is a practical, trustworthy way to use them on real work.

  • “Emails, reports and admin eat my whole day.”

    You learn to hand repeatable drafting, summarising and admin to AI, then review it in minutes instead of writing it from scratch.

  • “Everyone says use AI, but I don’t know where to start.”

    You learn to start with one task you already do, in plain language, with step-by-step guidance and no technical background.

  • “I tried it once. The answers were generic or wrong.”

    You learn to give AI the right context, and to check facts and tone before anything goes out under your name.

  • “My role is changing faster than my skills.”

    You build the AI literacy employers and clients now expect, with real outputs you can point to in your work or portfolio.

What changes for you

Skills that show up in your work the next day.

The goal is not to know every tool. It is to have a few reliable ways of working that save time and keep your judgment in charge.

  1. Hours back each week

    Repeatable drafting, research and admin move to AI, so your time goes to the work only you can do.

  2. Better work, checked

    Know what AI does well, where it fails, and how to review its output before it reaches a client or your manager.

  3. Proof you can show

    Leave with a workflow, a prototype or a plan you can bring to a performance review, a client or your portfolio.

  4. Relevant for what’s next

    Grow from a first workflow to bigger projects, at a pace that fits a full working week.

Before you start

Questions professionals ask

I’m not technical. Is this for me?

Yes. Faith Works is built for non-technical learners. You work in plain language on tasks you already do; any tool setup is explained step by step.

How much time do I need?

The Starter Kit takes about an hour. Workshops run for a few hours. Programs run over several weeks, and each program page shows the time commitment before you register.

Do I need paid AI tools?

Most first workflows work with free versions. If a program needs a paid account, its page says so before you sign up.

Can my employer pay for my place?

Often, yes. Programs list their fees once confirmed, and we can provide the details your employer needs. For a whole team, see For Business.

How is this different from For Business?

For Professionals is for you as an individual: your skills, your workflows, your career. For Business is for organizations that want training and adoption support designed around their teams and priorities.

What if I’m not sure where to start?

Start with the free Starter Kit, or send us a message about the work you want to improve and we will point you to the right next step.
