Make lead follow-up more consistent.

[ Workflow ]

From manual research to an AI-assisted research workflow.

  1. 01ResearchGather sources with AI search and summaries, and keep links to the originals.
  2. 02OrganizeGroup findings by theme so nothing important gets lost.
  3. 03AnalyzeAsk AI to compare, spot patterns and list open questions.
  4. 04DraftTurn the analysis into a first draft in your own structure.
  5. 05ReviewCheck facts and sources yourself. This step stays human.

Where it helps

Market scans, competitor notes, proposal research, briefing documents: any task where you read a lot to write a little.

Where to be careful

AI can state things confidently that aren't true. Keep sources attached at every step, and never skip the review.
