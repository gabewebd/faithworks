A simple starting plan

  1. Pick one repetitive task that takes too much time.
  2. Find an accessible tool to simplify or automate it. Check what you already pay for first.
  3. Test it, review every result, and improve it before you automate the next task.

Before you upload documents or customer details to an AI tool, check its data policy and your organization’s permissions. Keep a person on anything sensitive, complex or high-value.
