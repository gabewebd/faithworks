How an agent works

A goal, a few steps, the right tools, and you at the end

1. Goal

You describe the outcome and the limits.

2. Plan

It breaks the goal into steps.

3. Act

It uses a tool you approved.

4. Check

It reads the result and picks the next step.

Repeats until the task is done, or it gets stuck

5. You review

Nothing important is sent, paid or deleted without your yes.

What AI literacy looks like

Five everyday habits. None of them are technical.

Working AI literacy

Choose the task

Drafts and summaries, yes. Final decisions, no.

Write a brief

Who it’s for, what it’s for, how long.

Give context

It only knows what you paste in.

Check it

Facts, names, numbers, dates, tone.

Protect data

Know what may be shared, and where.

Before you speed anything up

Three questions that sort a task into one of four groups

Does this task still serve anyone?

Stop

Drop it. Automating waste only makes it faster.

Does its value come from you doing it personally?

Protect

Keep it human. Give it more time, not less.

Does it follow a pattern, and is it easy to check?

Anatomy of a useful prompt

Five parts that turn a vague request into a useful one

Works the same in any AI assistant
Help me turn the notes below into a clear action plan1 for a non-technical team.2 Group tasks by priority, identify missing information, and list assumptions.3 Do not invent details.4 Notes: [paste non-sensitive notes]5
1TaskWhat you want done, in one plain sentence.
2AudienceWho will read or use the result.
3FormatThe shape you want back: list, table, email.
4LimitsWhat it must not do, add or assume.
5MaterialThe notes or text it should work from.
