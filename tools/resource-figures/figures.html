How an agent works

A goal, a few steps, the right tools, and you at the end

1. Goal

You describe the outcome and the limits.

2. Plan

It breaks the goal into steps.

3. Act

It uses a tool you approved.

4. Check

It reads the result and picks the next step.

Repeats until the task is done, or it gets stuck

5. You review

Nothing important is sent, paid or deleted without your yes.

The billboard test

Before you paste, picture it on a billboard

Would I be fine seeing this on a billboard by the highway?

Your name, a client’s details, your password, your company’s plans, in letters three metres high.

If yes

Paste it

Public, general or made-up information.

If you’re not sure

Redact it first

Swap names and numbers for placeholders, or use a tool your work has approved.

If no

Keep it out

Passwords, codes, card numbers, IDs, other people’s private details.

A habit, not a prediction: most prompts never become public. The test protects you on the day one does.

What to share, and where

Sort information into three groups before you paste

Never paste

  • Passwords and PINs
  • One-time and recovery codes
  • API keys and access tokens
  • Card and bank account numbers
  • Passport, SSS, TIN and other ID numbers
  • Someone else’s health or legal details

Approved tools only

  • Customer and employee records
  • Contracts and pricing
  • Financial reports and forecasts
  • Unpublished plans and strategy
  • Source code and system details
  • Internal emails and reports

Usually fine

  • Public information
  • General questions
  • Your own writing, names removed
  • Made-up or sample data
  • Published articles and reports
  • Templates and outlines

Not sure which group? Treat it as the stricter one.
