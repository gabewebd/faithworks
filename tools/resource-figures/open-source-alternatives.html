Free to download is not always free

Four ways open-source tools can still cost you

Your laptop’s time

AI that runs on your own computer is free, but slower on an everyday laptop.

VoiceStudio took 3–7 seconds to make each second of audio.

Pay per use

Some apps are free, but every result runs on a paid AI service.

Open Higgsfield: about $0.25 for one 5-second video clip.

Your own server

Self-hosting is free software, but someone pays for and looks after the server.

Chatwoot asks for at least 2 CPU cores and 4 GB of RAM.

Licence limits

Free for you doesn’t always mean free for client or company work.

VoiceStudio’s main voice model is non-commercial; Cap Free is personal use.
