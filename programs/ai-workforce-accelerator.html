Who it’s for

You have tried AI tools, but they have not become part of how the work actually gets done.

  • Professionals new to AI
  • Founders and small business owners
  • Team leads who want a practical starting point

What you’ll learn

  1. Assess how ready your current workflow is for AI support
  2. Choose one recurring, reviewable process to improve
  3. Design the workflow: inputs, AI-assisted steps, human checks and output
  4. Build and test a first prototype of that workflow

What you leave with

  • A workflow readiness map
  • A documented workflow design
  • A first working prototype to test and refine

How it runs

The outline below is the planned flow. Session dates and durations are confirmed with each intake.

  1. 01

    Readiness

    What AI can and cannot do in your role, and where human judgment matters.

  2. 02

    Process selection

    Compare recurring tasks by frequency, effort, risk and ease of review.

  3. 03

    Workflow design

    Map inputs, AI-assisted steps, human checks and the final output.

  4. 04

    First prototype

    Build a first version, test it on a low-risk example and record what to improve.

Requirements and scope

What you need

  • A laptop and internet connection
  • One recurring task from your own work to practise on
  • Tool accounts as listed in the final program outline

Not included

  • Building a production system for your business
  • Paid software subscriptions, unless stated in the final outline

Questions about this program

Do I need a technical background?

No. The program is designed for non-technical learners. The final outline will list any preparation needed.

Will I leave with a finished system?

You leave with a first prototype and a plan to improve it. Turning it into a supported production system is a separate step.
