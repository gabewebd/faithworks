A little clarity before you begin

Good questions.
Clear next steps.

Do I need a technical background?

Many introductory programs are designed for nontechnical learners. Each program will specify its prerequisites so you can choose the right starting point.

What will I actually create?

It depends on the program. Learning projects may include a workflow plan, content system, landing page or business-assistant prototype. The confirmed program outline will specify the output and support.

Can you customize training for our company?

Yes. Start with your team size, goals, and preferred format. These help shape a relevant corporate proposal.

Do I need paid AI tools?

Requirements vary. Each program will identify the accounts, subscriptions and equipment needed before you register.

How can I join the next workshop?

Upcoming dates and registration details will appear when confirmed. For this design preview, use the inquiry builder to prepare your interests for the Faith Works team.
