A little clarity before you begin

Good questions.
Clear next steps.

Do I need a technical background?

Many introductory programs are designed for non-technical learners. Check each program’s prerequisites before registering.

What do I need to bring?

Hands-on sessions may require a laptop, internet access and tool accounts. The event page will list the exact requirements and any paid subscriptions.

Can you train our company?

Yes. Submit your team size, goals and preferred schedule through the corporate inquiry form.

Will I leave with a finished system?

Outcomes vary by program. Each program page specifies whether you are building a prototype, learning a workflow, or receiving implementation support.
