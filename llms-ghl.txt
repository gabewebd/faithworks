# Faith Works

> Faith Works (Faith Works PH) helps founders, professionals and teams work better with AI, without becoming tech experts, through practical workshops, guided programs, corporate training and business support. Founded by Faith Natividad, AI strategist, entrepreneur, international keynote speaker and content creator.

Faith Works teaches AI in plain language and focuses on applying it to the work people already do: choosing one useful workflow, testing a first version and keeping a person in charge. Motto: Learn. Implement. Scale.

Contact: ibelieve@faithph.ai
