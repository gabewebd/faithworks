[ 04 · Why it exists ]

Practical education, business transformation and purposeful growth.

AI is changing how work gets done, and most people are left to figure it out alone, between everything else on their plate.

Faith Works exists to make that practical: to teach AI, business and leadership in a way people can use the next day, and to keep them growing with purpose after the session ends.
