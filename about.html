Faith Natividad

Faith helps founders, professionals and organizations apply AI to practical work, through hands-on workshops, structured programs and one-to-one coaching.

Founder notes & behind-the-scenes

    Faith is the Founder & Creator of Faith Works + Faith AI, an ecosystem helping founders, professionals, and organizations grow through AI, systems, and human-centered leadership.

    Founder story

    She is also a multi-passionate content creator, business coach, and international keynote speaker on a mission to make AI practical, accessible, and profitable for entrepreneurs.

    Through Faith Works, she equips business owners worldwide with mindset, leadership, content, and growth strategies for the future of work. Through Faith AI, she helps founders implement AI-powered systems that automate marketing, sales, client communication, operations, and customer experience.

    Before building Faith Works + Faith AI, Faith founded Digital Workforce Group (DWG) during the pandemic, created global opportunities for Filipino professionals and helping businesses across six continents access recruitment, outsourcing, and operational support.

    To date, Faith has educated and mentored more than 4,000 entrepreneurs, executives, and professionals through trainings, business accelerators, keynote talks, workshops, and hands-on AI implementation programs.

    Her work bridges business, content, people, and technology helping founders build brands with authority, businesses with systems, and communities with purpose.

    Helping founders build businesses that work, even when they’re not.
